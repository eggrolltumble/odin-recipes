Tamagoyaki

Description

The classic (oishii!) Japanese omelet recipe that I specifically whipped up during my time living in the countryside of Japan. It's quite easy to do! Let's get rolling.

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp of soy sauce
  • 1 tsp of dry dashi
  • 1 tbsp of mirin
  • 1 tbsp of water
  • Drop of honey
  • 1 pack of seaweed

Steps

  1. Make dashi with 1 tbsp of water
  2. Beat eggs in a mixing bowl with the ingredients
  3. ◼️🔥 Heat the pan on low-medium heat
  4. 🥣🧻 Tear a paper towel into a bowl of oil for dabbing
  5. Dab oil on the pan
  6. Pour the sauce on the pan
  7. Use a spatula to flip 3-4 times
  8. Dab oil on the pan
  9. Pour the sauce on the pan
  10. 🍘 Put the nori seaweed on the egg
  11. Use a spatula to flip 3-4 times
  12. Dab oil on the pan
  13. Repeat steps 8-11 until done 👌

Don't forget to dab oil!

Top with Japanese mayo, sesame seeds, and cheese if you want.
