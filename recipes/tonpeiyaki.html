Tonpeiyaki

Description

My other favorite T-yaki meal that consists of shredded cabbage, your meat of choice wrapped inside a soft thin egg omelette. My favorite part about this one is the okonomiyaki drizzle and the aonori sprinkle✨

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp whole milk
  • 100 g thin-sliced pork belly (about 4 slices)
  • 50 g shredded green cabbage
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped green onion
  • 1 tsp cooking oil
  • 1 pinch salt and pepper
  • ½ tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ½ tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Japanese mayonnaise
  • 1 pinch aonori (dried green laver powder)

Steps

  1. Mix the Worcestershire sauce, oyster sauce, ketchup, honey, and soy sauce in a bowl to make the okonomiyaki sauce
  2. In another bowl, whisk the eggs and milk until smooth
  3. Heat a pan on medium and drizzle in the oil
  4. Add the cabbage and green onion, season with salt and pepper, and stir for 1 minute until softened
  5. Set the cabbage aside on a plate
  6. In the same pan, cook the pork belly until crispy on both sides
  7. Move the pork to the cabbage plate and wipe off excess oil from the pan
  8. Reduce the heat to medium-low and pour in the egg mixture
  9. Swirl the pan to keep the omelette fluffy
  10. Once the edges set, add the cabbage and pork
  11. When the omelette is about 80% cooked, carefully fold the edges over
  12. Flip it onto a plate and reshape with plastic wrap if needed
  13. Drizzle with the okonomiyaki sauce and add the mayo
  14. 🔑 Sprinkle with aonori

Enjoy!
