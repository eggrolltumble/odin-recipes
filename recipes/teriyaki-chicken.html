Teriyaki Chicken

Description

No! Teriyaki chicken is not Chinese food! "Teri" is the Japanese word for glazed and "yaki" means grill. It's been my favorite healthy protein to cook while living in Japan. It's so easy too!

Ingredients

  • 1 Chicken thigh (skin on)
  • 1 tbsp potato starch or corn starch
  • ½ tbsp oil
  • Salt (just a sprinkle✨)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 2 tbsp sake
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1 tsp dashi
  • 1 tsp honey

Steps

  1. Defrost the chicken
  2. 🥔🥣 Put 1 tbsp of potato starch or corn starch in a bowl
  3. 🥣 Add the soy sauce, mirin, sake, water, dashi, and honey to make the sauce
  4. 🧻 Dab the chicken with a paper towel to dry off the water
  5. 🔪 Make diagonal cuts on the meat side to even out the thick parts
  6. 🍳 Put ½ tbsp of oil in the pan
  7. 🧂 Sprinkle salt on the oil in the pan and on the meat side of the chicken
  8. Place the chicken skin side down on the oil
  9. ꩜🥢 Move the chicken around the pan in a circular motion to spread the oil evenly
  10. 🔥 Turn the heat on to medium
  11. Set a timer for 7 minutes
  12. Move the chicken around the pan as it cooks
  13. After 7 minutes, flip the chicken thigh over
  14. 🔥 Start a 2-minute timer
    1. 🧻 Wipe the oil off the pan with chopsticks and a paper towel
    2. 🥢 Poke holes in the chicken with chopsticks so the sauce soaks in
  15. 🥔🥣 After 2 minutes, pour the sauce and potato starch mixture over the chicken
  16. 🥄 Scoop the sauce over the chicken while it cooks
  17. 🔥 Cook until the sauce thickens
  18. 🔪 Cut into the chicken to make sure it's cooked through
  19. ✄ Use scissors to cut the chicken into slices

Enjoy!
